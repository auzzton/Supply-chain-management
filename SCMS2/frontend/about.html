About

Overview

The Supply Chain Management System (SCMS) is a comprehensive solution designed to streamline and optimize supply chain operations. Our system provides end-to-end visibility and control over inventory, orders, suppliers, and warehouses.

Key Features

Inventory Management

Track and manage inventory levels across multiple warehouses with real-time updates and automated alerts.

Order Processing

Efficiently process and track orders from creation to delivery with comprehensive order management.

Supplier Management

Maintain supplier relationships and track performance with detailed supplier profiles and analytics.

Warehouse Management

Optimize warehouse operations with location tracking and efficient space utilization.
